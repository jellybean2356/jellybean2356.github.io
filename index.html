about jellybean

jellybean is a mechatronics student at a secondary technical school in myjava (slovak city). in his free time, he taught himself several programming languages, including c#, python, html, css, javascript, react, and the basics of c and c++. one of his projects is the very website you’re browsing right now — the rest can be found in the projects section.

he got his first 3d printer, an ender 5 plus, about five years ago. since then, he has learned how to design 3d models in onshape, maintain the printer, and prepare models for printing. one of his biggest passions is creating video games in the unity game engine. none of his games are released yet, but some demos might appear in the projects section in the future.

he also enjoys tinkering with electronics. his first project was designing a custom macropad from scratch — and it turned out to be a success, which isn’t bad for a first try. outside of computers, he likes to go biking on longer rides, hiking, or just spending time in nature. of course, he’s also a gamer — some of his favorite games are valorant, cs2, terraria, and minecraft.
